# Not in the manuscript

Analyses that were run and are sound, but that do not appear in the paper.
Kept because the code exists and rerunning them is free; nothing here is cited
by main.tex and nothing here should be included in the supplement.

## SuppTable_TwoEnvironment_Heritability.csv

Produced by scripts/new_script/45_two_environment_heritability.R. Heritability
of each genotype's mean across the two trials and of the difference between
them, the latter being genotype-by-environment variance in single-kernel form.

Dropped from the manuscript because it has no claim of its own. Its conclusion,
that genotypes differ more in the between-trial change than in the within-trial
level, restates the CTL/LIN heritability contrast already reported, and the one
quantity the joint fit could have added, the genetic correlation between trials,
is not estimable here because no species clears the interval criterion in both.
